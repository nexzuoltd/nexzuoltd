1. Introduction

NEXZUO LTD ("we", "our", or "us") develops and publishes mobile games for Android via Google Play. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our applications, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By downloading or using any of our games, you agree to this policy. If you disagree, please uninstall the application and do not use our services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Device information — device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
  • Usage data — game progress, session length, in-game events, and feature interactions collected via analytics SDKs.
  • Advertising identifiers — the Android Advertising ID (AAID) used for showing relevant advertisements, where applicable.
  • Crash reports — diagnostic data generated automatically when an app crashes, to help us fix bugs.
  • Voluntarily submitted data — email address or other details you provide when contacting our support team.

We do not collect your name, address, payment details, or any government-issued ID numbers through our games.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve our games and services.
  • To analyse gameplay trends and fix technical issues.
  • To serve advertisements and measure ad performance (where our games are ad-supported).
  • To respond to support requests you submit by email.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Third-Party Services

Our games may integrate third-party SDKs for analytics and advertising. These providers may independently collect data subject to their own privacy policies. Common third-party services we may use include:

  • Google Play Services & Google AdMob
  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Unity Analytics / Unity Ads

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

5. Children's Privacy

Important: Our games are not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately at nexzuoltd@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

6. Data Retention

We retain collected data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. Aggregated analytics data may be retained for longer in anonymised form.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Opt out of personalised advertising via your device's advertising settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID).
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at nexzuoltd@gmail.com.

9. International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data during any international transfer.
